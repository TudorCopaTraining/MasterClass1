An Interactive Case Study Guide on Ethics & Trust in Artificial Intelligence

📖 Activity Directions & Objective

Objective: Learn to identify ethical failures in modern technological deployments by diagnosing real-world simulation scenarios.

  • Step 1: Read the brief definitions in the Core Principles Reference Guide below to familiarise yourself with the six pillars.
  • Step 2: Scroll down to the Interactive Case Simulator and read the presented case scenario carefully.
  • Step 3: Click on the AI principle that you think was neglected or violated in that scenario.
  • Step 4: Review the immediate feedback and explanation to understand why that pillar applies.
  • Step 5: Click "Next Scenario" to proceed through all 6 cases and review your final evaluation score.

Core Principles Reference Guide

⚖️ Fairness

AI systems should treat all human populations equitably and avoid bias across demographic groups.

🛡️ Reliability & Safety

AI systems should operate reliably, safely, and consistently under normal and unexpected conditions.

🔒 Privacy & Security

AI systems should respect user privacy laws and securely defend personal user data assets.

🔍 Transparency

AI systems should be explainable, allowing humans to understand exactly how algorithmic options are decided.

📋 Accountability

The engineers and organizations deploying AI frameworks must remain answerable for their direct real-world impacts.

🌍 Inclusiveness
