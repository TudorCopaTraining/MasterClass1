The Current Mess: The Expense Claims Pipeline

Right now, company employees email unformatted PDF receipts straight to HR. A human team member manually downloads each file, reads the raw text line-by-line, types the data fields into a massive Excel tracker spreadsheet, drafts an email to the employee's line manager for authorization, waits days for a response, and then manually keys the final data into the payroll software tool. It takes weeks, invites errors, and costs the business thousands in lost productivity.

🎯

Your Consulting Brief

Form teams of 3–4. Your objective is to entirely redesign this manual system. Architect an enterprise-ready workflow mapping that utilizes **AI Data Extraction**, **RPA Software Bots**, and critical **Human-in-the-Loop** safety checkpoints.
