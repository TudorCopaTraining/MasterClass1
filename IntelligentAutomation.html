Apprentice Workshop

The "Broken Process" Hackathon

Analyze real-world inefficiencies and architect a modern Intelligent Automation (IA) solution.

🚨 The Challenge Scenario: The Expense Claims Mess

Right now, employees email PDF receipts to HR. A human worker manually reads each PDF, extracts the data into a spreadsheet, drafts an email to the line manager for approval, waits for a reply, and then manually re-types the approved data into the company payroll system. It takes weeks, errors are common, and workers hate it.

Your Mission:

Work in teams of 3–4 to eliminate this manual grind by architecting an Intelligent Automation solution using AI/OCR, RPA, and Human-in-the-Loop safeguards.

⏱️ Step 1: Form Teams & Assign Roles (15 Mins)

Split into groups of 3 to 4. To simulate a real enterprise automation delivery team, assign one person to each of the following roles:

💼 Business Analyst Focuses on finding process bottlenecks, building the workflow steps, and mapping business value.
🛠️ Automation Architect Decides which tools (AI, RPA, or APIs) are right for each specific step of the process.
🛡️ Risk & Governance Officer Ensures compliance, data security, and designs the "Human-in-the-Loop" validation steps.

⏱️ Step 2: Bottleneck Hunting (30 Mins)

Sketch out the current manual workflow on paper, a physical whiteboard, or a digital canvas like Miro. Identify exactly where the process slows down or suffers from human error.

💡 Quick Cheat Sheet for Apprentices:

• Rule-based & Repetitive: Perfect for standard RPA (e.g., copying data from a cell to an application form).

• Unstructured Data & Cognitive judgment: Requires AI (e.g., extracting items from a poorly scanned image or photo of a receipt).

⏱️ Step 3: Design the Architecture (45 Mins)

Build your new automated ecosystem. Your final technical design blueprint must use all three fundamental layers of Intelligent Automation:

1. Data Ingestion (AI / OCR Layer)

How will your system read the unstructured receipt? (e.g., An AI model that reads text from images and structures it into JSON formats).

2. Process Execution (RPA / Integration Layer)

What software robots or API integrations will move the data to where it needs to live without human intervention?

3. Human-in-the-Loop (Governance Layer)

Where should the robot stop and ask a human manager for a manual review? Set a specific rule boundary (e.g., financial value limits or low confidence scores).
