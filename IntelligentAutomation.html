🚀 Interactive Apprentice Challenge

The "Broken Process" Hackathon

Step into the shoes of an automation consultant. Deconstruct enterprise inefficiencies and design a modern, scalable Intelligent Automation (IA) ecosystem.

🚨

The Challenge: The Expense Claims Mess

Right now, employees email chaotic PDF receipts to HR. A human worker manually downloads each document, reads the text, copies variables into a massive Excel spreadsheet, drafts an email to a line manager for approval, waits days for a reply, and then manually types the data into the company payroll software. It takes weeks, errors occur constantly, and employees hate the wait.

🎯

Your Mission Architecture

Work in teams of 3–4 to entirely eliminate this manual grind. Architect an end-to-end automation system balancing AI Extraction, RPA Bots, and Human-in-the-Loop risk boundaries.

⏱️

Step 1: Form Teams & Align Roles (15 Mins)

To simulate a real enterprise automation center of excellence (CoE), divide your squad and assign one person to each core technical pillar:

💼 Business Analyst

Responsible for locating process bottlenecks, tracking task times, and validating final operational business value.

🛠️ Automation Architect

Decides the technological design stack. Maps out where to drop AI models vs. where to use light software bots.

🛡️ Governance & Risk

Secures employee data privacy, handles risk metrics, and designs rules for human intervention thresholds.

⏱️

Step 2: Bottleneck Mapping Canvas (30 Mins)

Sketch out the current workflow step-by-step on a board. Pinpoint the slow, high-friction areas. Use this visual guide rulebook to categorize the architecture paths:

✔ Rule-Based Tasks

Predictable, structured, repetitive data steps (e.g., copying plain text rows from Excel). Ideal for standard RPA Bots.

🧠 Cognitive Judgments

Unstructured inputs, images, handwriting, or context choice evaluation. Requires an **AI / Machine Learning** API node.

⏱️
